STORM PATH PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 31 August 2026. Storm Path is a weather-aware navigation app. This policy describes what the iOS and Android apps collect and why.

Location

Storm Path requests foreground location only. The first finite GPS fix is the only legal trigger for National Weather Service and NOAA radar requests. Location is used on-device to center the map, request public weather, and route a drive. Location is not sold. Location is not used for advertising. Background tracking is not required for the current AND-gate.

Weather and radar

Public endpoints are used: https://api.weather.gov/ and NOAA OpenGeo WMS base reflectivity. Those services receive the live coordinate needed to return a forecast, alerts, or a radar image. No Storm Path account is attached to those calls.

On-device storage

Recent destinations, saved Home/Work, and unit settings stay on this device via private app storage. There is no cloud account in version 1.0.0.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or phone number
  • Payment data
  • Contacts, photos, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking

Children

Storm Path is not directed at children under 13.
